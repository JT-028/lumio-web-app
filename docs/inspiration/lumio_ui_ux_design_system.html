UI / UX Design System

A cozy magical world for learning.

Lumio pairs a friendly owl wizard with personalized study guides, adaptive tutoring, meaningful practice, and mastery-based rewards.

Your little guide to big ideas.
01 / Foundation

Brand identity

Lumio should feel like stepping into a little magical library where every child has a personal owl mentor and a world of knowledge to explore. Keep the experience playful enough to invite return visits, but structured enough that parents and teachers trust it as a serious learning tool.

♡

Supportive

Never shames mistakes. Treats confusion as a normal part of learning.

✦

Magical

Makes discovery exciting through subtle stars, warm light, and storybook details.

🌱

Encouraging

Celebrates real improvement, persistence, and independent problem-solving.

📖

Intelligent

Explains concepts and offers useful next steps instead of simply scoring answers.

Design rule: Use the owl to express emotion, the interface to communicate information, and the reward system to reinforce learning. Magical effects should mark meaningful moments, not distract during lessons.

05 / Character language

Mascot system: Lumio as the tutor

The owl is an active teaching companion, not just decoration. Its expression should reinforce the purpose of the dialogue.

Reaction IDPurposeExample dialogue
greetingWelcome“Hi there! Ready to discover something new?”
teachingExplain concepts“Let's look at this one step at a time.”
thinkingHints and reasoning“What do you notice about these two numbers?”
listeningStudent questions“I'm listening. What would you like to know?”
encouragingMistakes or difficulty“That's okay! Mistakes help us learn. Let's try another way.”
celebratingMastery and achievements“You figured it out! You can solve this kind of problem on your own!”

welcomingReturning from a break“Welcome back! Shall we continue our adventure?”
Implementation: select mascot expressions using stable reaction identifiers in tutor response metadata. Export each pose as an individual transparent asset with consistent framing and scale.

07 / Progression

Gamification that supports mastery

01 · Learn

Understand a new concept.

02 · Practice

Apply it to questions.

03 · Master

Demonstrate understanding.

04 · Unlock

Earn rewards and new challenges.

Reward mechanics

MechanicGuideline
XPReward meaningful practice, learning activities, and demonstrated improvement.
Skill levelsReflect progress in individual topics, not just time spent.
BadgesRecognize persistence, improvement, and independent problem-solving.
StreaksEncourage consistency while allowing breaks without harsh penalties.
UnlockablesOffer owl accessories, library decorations, or map areas.
Mastery milestonesRequire demonstrated understanding across relevant questions, possibly over multiple sessions.
Daily goalsOffer small, achievable goals based on student needs and available time.
Core rule: Don't award more XP just because a student clicks faster or submits more answers. Reward meaningful attempts, corrections, and learning progress. Avoid punitive streaks, shaming leaderboards, and pay-to-win mechanics.
08 / Dialogue

AI tutoring conversation

Lumio should guide students toward understanding rather than immediately revealing every answer.

LUMIO

Let's solve this together! What is 12 ÷ 3? Imagine sharing 12 stars equally among 3 friends.

STUDENT

Is it 4?

LUMIO

That's right! Each friend gets 4 stars. You found the answer by sharing equally. Want to try one with a bigger number?

  • Keep explanations short, friendly, and focused on one idea.
  • Offer worked examples, visual analogies, or simpler wording.
  • Use guiding questions before revealing answers when appropriate.
  • Acknowledge frustration and suggest a manageable next step.
  • Keep the study guide and current question accessible during tutoring.
  • Check generated explanations for age appropriateness and accuracy.

10 / Inclusive design

Accessibility, child safety & responsive UX

Accessible by default

  • Important touch targets at least 44 × 44 CSS px
  • Readable text and strong contrast
  • Keyboard access for navigation, quizzes, and dialogs
  • Reduced-motion option
  • Sound effects optional and muted by default
  • Feedback uses text and icons, not color alone

Safe learning experience

  • Collect only necessary student data
  • Offer appropriate parent and teacher oversight
  • Clearly identify Lumio as an AI assistant
  • Allow questionable explanations to be flagged
  • Avoid pressure tactics and punitive streaks
  • Use guided navigation for younger learners

12 / Delivery plan

Recommended implementation order

  1. Foundation: Typography, colors, buttons, cards, responsive navigation, mascot asset pipeline.
  2. Core learning: Home dashboard, study-guide generation, saved guides, practice questions, AI tutoring.
  3. Learning feedback: Hints, explanations, topic progress, review recommendations, mastery checks.
  4. Gamification: XP, badges, unlockables, quest maps, meaningful celebrations.
  5. Oversight and refinement: Parent/teacher summaries, privacy settings, accessibility testing, evaluation with intended users.
